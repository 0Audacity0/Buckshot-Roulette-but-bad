Shotbuck Game

(so cool tutorial)

The Dealer sits across the table. Between you and The Dealer, there lies a shotgun, loaded with a hidden mix of LIVE and BLANK shells. You each have 5 charges . Once you hit zero, you're done. Survive at least three rounds.

  • On your turn, shoot The Dealer or shoot yourself .
  • Shoot yourself with a blank and you go again. A live shell takes 1 charge away from you.
  • Shoot The Dealer with a blank and the turn passes onto The Dealer.
  • Win a round and a briefcase with $300 slides onto the table. Spend the cash you get on items from the store after you win a round, or on the title screen (you have 5 slots). Every time you buy one, The Dealer gets one too, just to make it 'fair'.

Items (click them on the table or use the buttons / keys 1-5):
🔍 Magnifying Glass, $50: see if the chamber is live or blank
🍺 Beer, $100: restores 1 charge
🔪 Knife, $150: cut the barrel so a live shot takes 2 charges

Controls: click the Dealer or press W to shoot him · press S to shoot yourself · move the mouse to look around. On a phone or tablet: use the buttons, or tap an item to use it (tap the Dealer twice to shoot him).
